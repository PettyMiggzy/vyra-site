Who is VYRA

VYRA is the mascot of VYRE, the chain where gas is USDC. Half-lidded eyes, a quiet smile, a black ninja outfit and neon-lime wraps on the wrist and neck.

The V in its hand is the V in VYRE. VYRA holds it out like a business card. That is the whole story, and it is enough.
